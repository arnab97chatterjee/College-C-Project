1. Problem statement and required results
Develop an employee payroll management system that stores employee information, attendance, salary components, allowances and deductions; calculates gross and net salary using configurable payroll rules; generates individual salary slips; and maintains monthly payroll records.
•	Maintain employee ID, designation, department and basic salary.
•	Record attendance and applicable leave information.
•	Calculate allowances, bonuses and deductions.
•	Compute gross salary and net payable salary.
•	Generate employee-wise salary slips.
•	Produce monthly payroll summaries and department-wise salary reports.
•	Keep employee, attendance and payroll records permanently so they remain available after closing and reopening the program.
